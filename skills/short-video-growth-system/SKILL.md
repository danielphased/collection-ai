# Short-video growth system

Distribution beats another feature. The system is small on purpose: one free
thing people want, seven short videos, one call to action.

Project-specific facts (product, audience, brand voice, channels) belong in the project's own CLAUDE.md — use them when present, and ask for the product and the audience if they are missing.

## The system

1. **One free thing.** A small tool or resource the audience already wants and
   that leads naturally into the product: a calculator, a checker, a template,
   a question bank. It must be useful without signing up for anything else.
2. **Seven videos, one afternoon.** Film a week of videos in one session and schedule them.
3. **One problem per video.** Each video answers a single real question from
   one kind of customer, in their words.
4. **One call to action.** Each video ends by asking viewers to comment one
   keyword to get the free thing. Comments also widen the video's reach.
5. **Automated delivery.** A commenter receives the link, lands on a short
   page, gets the free thing and is offered the product.

## What to produce

| Deliverable | Contents |
|---|---|
| Free-tool brief | What it does, the one input and one output, how it hands over to the product |
| Seven scripts | Table below, one per video, 20 to 45 seconds each |
| Batch plan | Shot list, order of filming, props or screens needed, posting schedule |
| Landing page copy | Headline, three lines, one field, what happens next |
| Follow-up | Delivery message plus at most two follow-up emails |
| Funnel estimate | Formula below, filled with the user's own numbers |

Script shape for each video:

| Part | Seconds | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 | The customer's question, in their words |
| Answer | 3–30 | The actual answer, with one concrete number or example |
| Proof | optional | The free tool on screen, doing it |
| Call to action | last 3–5 | "Comment KEYWORD and I'll send it" |

Source the seven questions from real material: support tickets, sales calls,
reviews of competitors, forum threads. Do not invent customer pain.

## Funnel estimate

```
sign-ups per video = views × comment rate × delivery rate × sign-up rate
```

Fill it with the account's own history. Without history, state the estimate as
a range and call it a guess. Figures quoted in growth videos (tens of
thousands of views, 1% commenting, 40% of commenters signing up) are
illustrations, not benchmarks; views per video is the hard part.

Track per video: views, comments, messages delivered, landing-page visits,
sign-ups. Keep the two best questions and replace the rest next week.

## Rules

- **Honest hooks.** No fake scarcity, fake results or claims without a source. The free thing must be what the video promised.
- **Platform rules.** Automated comment replies and direct messages are restricted on every platform. Use an approved tool, respect its limits, and check the platform's current rules before switching it on.
- **Consent.** Collect an email only with clear consent, send what was promised, and make unsubscribing work. For EU audiences apply GDPR; marketing consent is separate from delivery of the free thing. See the `pre-launch-compliance-check` skill.
- **Approval.** Draft everything; post, send and switch on automation only after the user approves.
- **One call to action per video.** Never two.
